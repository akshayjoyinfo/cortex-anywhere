cortex-lite · data model

Cortex Lite Data Ownership

Who owns what, where it is stored, and which key protects it. The customer signs the contract; the tenant is the isolation unit.

The boundary is the tenant, not the customer. Two tenants of the same customer are isolated from each other like strangers. The CI leak test seeds exactly that case.
